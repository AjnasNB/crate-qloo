## Evidence and revisions

Nineteen distinct video criteria pass. FFmpeg decoded all 1,200 frames; automated checks cover timestamps, black intervals, visible nonuniform content, long freezes, format and duration. Hyperframes runtime/layout checks sample 42 timeline positions, including 25 transition positions. Selected designed-text contrast checks and caption reading-time checks pass. These are bounded checks, not nineteen creative revision rounds or human visual inspection of every frame.

Independent visual review found two useful readability defects. The packing scene now displays all eight real solver placements in large, source-labeled lists. The evidence scene now enlarges the actual panel detail and pairs it with a readable status card. Stall headings align with their lists. Only those two seven-second segments were rendered again and stitched into the final forty-second file. Representative frames decoded from the final MP4 were inspected.

The repair example uses the copied exact planner: twelve titles, twenty copies, two shop reserves; removing Piranesi replaces it with The Lathe of Heaven and preserves seven of eight placements. Rankings remain synthetic. No live API result, external language-model call, individual attendee preference, sales lift or customer testimonial is presented.

The source retains seven nonblocking timeline-structure warnings and documented intentional image crops. Local preview is a convenience; final encoded-video QA is separate from Studio loading. The public ZIP excludes credentials, internal instructions, caches, private captures, intermediary videos and proprietary font programs. Our code includes the MIT license; third-party code retains its included notices.
