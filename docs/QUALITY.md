# Crate quality record

Developed by Ajnas N B

The review target is a useful, truthful, working product. Repeated checks do not establish a winning probability or perfection. Existing evidence is reused when its inputs have not changed; new failures trigger focused revisions and verification.

- [Sixteen concept review rounds](IDEATION.md): narrowing, criticism, competitors, physical constraints and the live-evidence gate.
- [Sixteen product checks](PRODUCT-QA.md): fixes and browser evidence for access, states, recovery, stock decisions and responsive use.
- [Sixteen technical checks](TECHNICAL-QA.md): allocator, CSV, Qloo boundary, MCP contracts, credentials and submission evidence.
- Video: separate local source and render in `D:\ARCPrize\qloo\video`; its QA record distinguishes scene samples, automated full-file checks and independent visual review. A finite sample must not be called an inspection of every rendered frame.

Current essential gates: a working server-side Qloo key, credible live candidate coverage/rankings, a successful live external-tool session and an actual Devpost submission receipt. Customer usefulness, measured time savings and sales effects remain unvalidated. Software tests and a polished video do not replace these gates.
