# Crate quality record

Developed by Ajnas N B

The review target is a useful, truthful, working product. Repeated checks do not establish a winning probability or perfection. Existing evidence is reused when its inputs have not changed; new failures trigger focused revisions and verification.

- [Sixteen concept review rounds](IDEATION.md): narrowing, criticism, competitors, physical constraints and the live-evidence gate.
- [Sixteen product checks](PRODUCT-QA.md): fixes and browser evidence for access, states, recovery, stock decisions and responsive use.
- [Sixteen technical checks](TECHNICAL-QA.md): allocator, CSV, Qloo boundary, MCP contracts, credentials and submission evidence.
- [Video: twenty-seven motion-version checks and published artifacts](VIDEO.md). The source and render are also local in `D:\ARCPrize\qloo\video`. The QA record distinguishes scene samples, automated full-file checks and independent visual review. A finite visual sample must not be called a visual inspection of every rendered frame.

Devpost confirmed submission on October 17, 2026 with the pending-live limitation disclosed. Current essential gates remain a working server-side Qloo key, credible live candidate coverage/rankings and a successful live external-tool session. Customer usefulness, measured time savings and sales effects remain unvalidated. Software tests, a submission receipt and a polished video do not replace these gates.
