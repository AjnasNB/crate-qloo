# One copy cannot leave twice

Developed by Ajnas N B

Problem: A recommendation is unusable when its single copy has already left in another crate.

Audience: Booksellers and judges watching a website demo, with sound or captions.

Goal: Understand one complete Crate workflow and try the current live product.

Emotion: Small packing tension becomes a calm, inspectable resolution.

One true thing: Actual Qloo order plus shared stock produces eight feasible placements. Removing The Dispossessed and locking Soul & City replaces only the missing title with Kindred.

## Ideas considered

1. Transformation chain: the logo unfolds into two crates. Rejected: the name's first association and a generic reveal.
2. UI documentary: a cursor performs pack, lock and repair entirely in captured UI. Specific, but the dense interface leaves the physical scarcity hard to feel.
3. A continuous packing-table case: one scarce book has two possible destinations; a live evidence macro motivates its path; a later vacancy is repaired without disturbing the other crate. Chosen because shared copies, exact title identities and protected placements are Crate's operational distinction.

Cover-the-logo test: a generic book recommender cannot tell the unchanged-seven-placement repair story. Competitor swap: a generic inventory video cannot replace the cross-domain live evidence beat.

## World and film system

One warm desk with a soft key from upper left, cloth/paper surfaces, real page-block depth and cast shadows. A continuous camera path supplies scale changes. Calm smooth frame clock at 30 fps; crisp physical motion with decelerating arrivals, no bounce loop. A single real 3D book is the hero, then the camera opens onto the shared table. Actual UI is a cropped, magnified photographic fragment. No header, footer, corner brand, progress chrome or stat wall. Sound is a measured local voice plus sparse material contact accents; no purchased score.

Screenshot-worthy hero: The Dispossessed suspended over its actual empty position, Soul & City's four books protected behind it, before Kindred settles into the vacancy.
