## The idea

One copy cannot leave in two crates. A single physical packing-table case makes Crate's shared-stock constraint visible, then performs the local repair: The Dispossessed sells out, Soul & City stays locked, and Kindred fills the vacant Future Frequencies slot. The other seven title/stall placements remain. The physical world belongs to this product's central action, rather than being a sequence of feature slides.

The alternative treatments were a logo transformation and a cursor-only UI tour. The first was generic; the second made the missing-copy consequence hard to see. The chosen film keeps one book, one table and one causal state change through the camera moves.
