## Remaining limits

The decoded every-second sheet, cut neighbours and key full frames received independent visual review with no remaining P0/P1. This does not mean every encoded frame was perceptually watched. The screenshot pan is the softest shot because it magnifies real browser pixels; it could benefit from a higher-resolution verified capture. The voice was locally transcribed and measured, not perceptually heard. Mean luma motion is below the skill's human-film reference band; the calm full-table reading pause is retained deliberately. Framework DOM motion/contrast warnings do not evaluate the WebGL world. These limits remain visible in the measured reports rather than being converted into success claims.
