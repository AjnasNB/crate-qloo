# Production specification

Developed by Ajnas N B

- Goal: make the missing-copy decision legible, then show that only its placement changes.
- Output: HyperFrames, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 995 frames / 33⅙ seconds, H.264 video and AAC sound below 24 MiB.
- Ground: #e6e9df studio desk, paper/page blocks and wood-grain crate slats. Key light from [-6,12,8], intensity 2.7; rim [9,7,-7], intensity 1.2; contact shadow opacity .30.
- Colour: actual book colours from sample.ts; #285544 packaging strap means protected. Cover faces preserve source hues; page blocks and geometry receive real scene lighting.
- Typography: app Georgia at 70–76 px for spoken beat copy, Segoe UI at 66 px for protection, actual captured UI magnified 4.25–5.2 times. Closing wordmark 180 px, developer credit 48 px.
- Objects: eight selected physical book copies, one replacement copy, two crates, real programme and evidence captures, actual Crate mark. No publisher artwork. Book geometry is 1.75 × 2.48 × .33 units with page edges.
- Motion: smooth 30 fps frame clock; softLand book arrivals over .70 s, placements stagger .24 s; one stock-out departure; one replacement arrival over 1.35 s. The other seven title/stall placements remain unchanged.
- Voice: locally generated af_heart stock voice at .90 speed. Cue boundaries are measured from the actual generated WAV segments. Original procedural contact sounds land at 13.5808, 21.7255 and 25.3581 seconds. No music bed.
- Shots: opening/scarcity 0–5.37; actual programme 5.37–7.47; live evidence 7.47–12.78; packing 12.78–17.13; departure 17.13–21.28; protection 21.28–24.01; replacement 24.01–28.28; resolved desk 28.28–30.34; close 30.34–33.17. Exact frame boundaries derive from narration timing and the source frame clock.
- Banned: corner labels, progress chrome, chapter counters, claims of sales or demand, prize odds, generic mock dashboards, publisher cover imitation, synthetic ranks passed off as live, fake customer events and repeated slide transitions.
- QA: every-second encoded contact sheet; full-resolution programme/live/stock-out/repair/close frames; all-frame decode and luma analysis; Motion sequential-versus-jumped frame test; measured audio and WebVTT timing; independent visual critique.
